Why Corning IT Solution

What it's actually like to work here.

Remote-first, flexible hours

Work from anywhere. We care about shipped, working software and clear communication — not seat time.

Real ownership, real clients

You'll work directly on live client products from day one, not a sandbox project — with your name on the commits.

Direct mentorship, small teams

No layers of management. You work closely with senior engineers who review your code and actually explain the why.

Breadth across the stack

Rotate across web, mobile, and ERP/Frappe engineering — most people here can speak to more than one part of the stack within a year.

Open Roles

We hire when the project calls for it.

We don't keep a standing pipeline — roles open up as new client work comes in, rather than on a fixed schedule. That said, we're always happy to hear from strong engineers, designers, and QA folks — good conversations sometimes turn into an offer months later when the right project comes up.
