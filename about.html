About Corning IT Solution

Years of turning ideas into shipped software.

We're a team of engineers, designers, and product thinkers who've spent years helping businesses build things worth using.

Our Story

Started small. Built to last.

Corning IT Solution was founded in 2019 by a small group of engineers who were tired of watching good ideas get stuck in bad software. We started with two clients and a shared laptop's worth of ambition.

Since then we've grown into a full-service development company — delivering 60+ projects across e-commerce, healthcare, logistics, fintech, and education — without losing the thing that got us here: we still ship code ourselves, and we still pick up the phone.

Today, Corning IT Solution is a registered Limited Liability Partnership with a distributed team of 20+ specialists across engineering, design, and delivery.

Our Mission

To help businesses move faster by building software that's reliable, maintainable, and genuinely enjoyable to use — without the agency overhead.

Our Vision

To be the long-term technology partner our clients call first — not just a vendor for a single project.
