Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Corning IT Solution LLP ("Corning IT Solution", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit citsolution.in or submit information through our contact form.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you fill out our contact form. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • Details about your project, budget range, and service interest

We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking on this site, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your project
  • Send you a proposal, quote, or follow-up related to your request
  • Maintain internal records of client communication

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-party processors

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which transmits your submission to us by email. Formspree may process your data in accordance with its own privacy policy. We use this service solely to route form submissions and do not share your data with any other third party.

5. Data retention

We retain information from your inquiry for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of our business relationship and as required for our accounting and legal record-keeping obligations.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us at contactus@citsolution.in. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, email us at contactus@citsolution.in.
