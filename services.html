Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a typical project take?+

Most websites take 4–10 weeks, mobile apps 8–16 weeks, and custom software 6–20 weeks — depending on scope. We give you a firm timeline after the discovery call.

Do you offer fixed-price or hourly engagements?+

Both. Well-defined projects usually run fixed-price with milestones; ongoing work and evolving products run on a dedicated-team or monthly retainer model.

Will you sign an NDA?+

Yes — we sign NDAs before any detailed discovery discussion if you'd like one in place.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes. Every project includes a warranty period, and we offer ongoing SLA-backed maintenance plans after that.

Can you migrate us from Excel or Tally to ERPNext?+

Yes — data migration from spreadsheets or Tally into ERPNext is one of our most common ERP engagements, including chart-of-accounts and historical transaction imports.

What if I only need one small feature added?+

Smaller, well-scoped changes fit naturally under our monthly retainer model rather than a full project engagement — no need to commission a whole new project for a small addition.
